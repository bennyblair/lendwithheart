How broker commissions work

Mortgage brokers are commonly paid by the lender. Your broker should disclose the commission and any fees before you proceed.

01

Upfront commission

This is a one-off payment a lender may pay the broker when your loan settles. It is commonly calculated as a percentage of the loan amount.

For Lend With Heart, this is the commission used to calculate your estimated charity impact.
02

Trail commission

This is an ongoing payment a lender may pay the broker while your loan remains active. It is commonly based on your outstanding loan balance.

Trail commission is separate from the upfront amount and should be disclosed clearly.

Good to know: Commission arrangements vary by lender and loan product. We’ll show you the applicable commission, any fees, and your confirmed charity amount before you proceed.
